How to Stay Safe Around "HokiArena" Lookalikes

Trust & Safety · Published July 2026 · Updated November 13, 2026

"Hoki" is a common word reused across a lot of unrelated apps and sites, some of them real-money products with no connection to this directory. (A small number of this directory's own listings are disclosed paid affiliate links that lead to real-money-adjacent sites — see Legalities — which is different from an unrelated app impersonating the Hoki Arena name.) This page covers general red flags worth knowing, regardless of which "Hoki"-named product you've come across.

Common red flags

  • Being asked to log in or register. This directory has no accounts — see HokiArena Login: Why There Isn't One.
  • Being asked to install an APK file. HokiArena distributes nothing to install — see HokiArena APK: What You Need to Know.
  • Being asked for a deposit or "top-up" balance on hokiarena.games itself. This directory's own domain never asks for money. (Some of our listings link out to third-party affiliate sites that may ask for money on their own domains — that's a choice you make on their site, not ours.)
  • Being contacted via WhatsApp, Telegram, or SMS with a "bonus" link. We don't distribute links through messaging apps — the only official entry point is browsing directly to hokiarena.games.

How to confirm you're on the real site

Check the address bar carefully — the official domain is hokiarena.games, nothing else. See Verify Official Site for a full step-by-step check.
