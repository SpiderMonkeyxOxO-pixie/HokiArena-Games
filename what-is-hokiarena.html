What Is HokiArena?

Guide · Updated for 2026

HokiArena — also written "Hoki Arena" — is a browser-based directory of casual mobile games at hokiarena.games. Occasionally mistyped as "hockey arena." If you landed here trying to figure out exactly what this site is, this page covers it directly.

What HokiArena actually is

HokiArena is a curated list of casual games, each with a short, honest write-up covering what it plays like and who it's for. Alongside the directory, there's a blog with guides and roundups. You browse it the same way you'd browse any website — no setup involved.

Not "hockey arena"

If autocomplete brought you here from "hockey arena," this isn't a sports app or booking site — it's a casual game directory. The name comes from "hoki," Indonesian slang for luck or fortune, not the sport. See Hockey Arena vs. HokiArena for the full breakdown.

What HokiArena is not

  • Not a site with a login. No account is required to browse. See HokiArena Login: Why There Isn't One.
  • Not an app or APK. There's no separate "HokiArena App" to download — everything runs in your browser. See HokiArena APK: What You Need to Know.
  • Not a gambling operator itself. Hoki Arena doesn't run wagering, deposits, or withdrawals, but some listings are paid affiliate links to third-party sites that may offer real-money features. See our Legalities page for the full disclosure.
  • Not affiliated with other "Hoki"-named products. The word gets reused a lot. See Verify Official Site.
