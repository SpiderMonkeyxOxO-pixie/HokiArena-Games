What's Changed on Hoki Arena

Running log · 2026

A short, dated log of meaningful changes to the directory — new listings, structural changes, and anything else worth flagging. For game-specific news and longer reads, see the blog.

2026

July 2026 — Site redesign

Hoki Arena was rebuilt from the ground up: new navigation (Home, Games, Blogs, About Us, Contact Us), a new mobile-first design, a filterable games directory, a blog, and full legal pages, including a clearer affiliate-link disclosure in our Legalities page.

July 2026 — Directory launched with 12 listings

The initial games directory launched with twelve listings across category placeholders spanning arcade, puzzle, racing, action, strategy, casual, adventure, and sports.

July 2026 — Blog launched

Added the first three articles: how we choose games for the directory, mobile gaming trends to watch in 2026, and a guide to casual vs. core games.

July 2026 — Resource pages added

Added App Info, Troubleshooting & FAQ, and Verify Official Site pages to answer common questions in one place.

July 2026 — Real game artwork, categories consolidated to six

Replaced placeholder icons across every listing with real game artwork, and rewrote several listings to match. Categories were consolidated from eight down to six — arcade, puzzle, racing, action, strategy, and adventure — to match what's actually listed.

July 2026 — Directory reset to ten listings, in progress

The directory has been reset to ten placeholder listings while the final lineup is reviewed. Individual detail pages now exist for each slot and will fill in with full write-ups, artwork, and descriptions as each game is confirmed. See Games for the current state.

July 2026 — Simplified to a single Casual category

Hoki Arena is a casual-games directory specifically, so the six-category structure was replaced with one: Casual. Category filter pills and category pages were removed in favor of a simple name search on the Games page.

July 2026 — HokiArena guide cluster added

Added a set of plain-language guides answering the questions people most often search alongside the HokiArena name: What Is HokiArena?, What Does HokiArena Mean?, HokiArena Login, HokiArena APK Guide, HokiArena Mobile App Guide, HokiArena Safety Guide, HokiArena User Guide 2026, HokiArena 2026 Guide, and a dedicated HokiArena FAQ.

Noticed something that needs updating? Let us know.
