Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Hoki Arena ("we", "us") collects when you visit this website, how it's used, and the choices available to you. Hoki Arena is an informational game directory — we do not operate any real-money or wagering activity ourselves. This policy covers only data collected on hokiarena.games; it does not cover the privacy practices of third-party sites reached through our affiliate links, which are governed by their own policies.

Information we collect

We keep the data we collect to a minimum:

  • Contact form submissions: If you use our contact form, the name, email address, and message you provide are sent to us so we can reply.
  • Basic usage data: Like most websites, our hosting and any analytics tools we use may automatically log standard technical information such as browser type, device type, approximate location (derived from IP address), and pages visited, for the purpose of understanding traffic and fixing issues.
  • Cookies: We may use minimal cookies or local storage to remember basic preferences (for example, keeping the mobile navigation menu working correctly). We do not use cookies to track you across other websites.

How we use information

  • To respond to messages sent through our contact form
  • To understand how visitors use the directory, so we can improve navigation and content
  • To maintain the security and reliability of the site

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use any data to power gambling, betting, or wagering features, because Hoki Arena does not operate any ourselves.

Third-party services

We may rely on third-party services for hosting, analytics, or email delivery. These providers process data on our behalf under their own privacy and security practices. We choose providers with reasonable data-handling standards, but we encourage you to review the policies of any linked third-party game, app, or store separately — those are outside our control.

Data retention

Contact form messages are kept only as long as needed to respond to and resolve your inquiry. Basic usage logs are retained for a limited period for security and troubleshooting purposes.

Your choices

  • You can decline to use the contact form if you'd prefer not to share your name or email.
  • Most browsers let you block or clear cookies and local storage at any time through their settings.
  • You can request that we delete any message you've previously sent us by contacting us directly.

Children's privacy

Hoki Arena is intended for a general audience and is not directed at young children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as the site changes. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.

Contact us

For privacy-related questions or requests, email hello@hokiarena.games or use our Contact Us page.
