Verify You're on the Official Hoki Arena Site

Reference page · Updated for 2026

"Hoki" is a common word used in the names of a lot of unrelated apps and sites in this space, including some offering real-money games. This page explains how to confirm you're actually looking at this directory, and what to watch for if something claiming to be "Hoki Arena" doesn't match what's described here. Note: this directory's own game listings do link out to third-party sites as paid affiliate placements, and some of those destination sites may themselves involve real-money features — see our Legalities page for the full disclosure. The checks below are about confirming you're on this domain, not a verdict on what every linked site does.

The official domain is hokiarena.games. This site never asks for payment details, deposits, or account top-ups, and never distributes an installable app.

Quick checks

  • Check the domain carefully. Look at the full address bar, not just the page title — copycat sites often use a similar name on a different domain, or a misspelled version of ours.
  • No login, no problem. This directory never requires an account. If a site or app using the Hoki Arena name is asking you to log in, register, or verify an account, it isn't this site. See HokiArena Login: Why There Isn't One.
  • No app, no APK. We don't distribute an installable app. If you're being asked to download an APK file "for Hoki Arena," treat that as unrelated to this directory — see HokiArena APK: What You Need to Know.
  • Hokiarena.games itself never asks for a deposit, a "top-up," or any payment, and never offers a withdrawal. If the exact domain in your address bar isn't hokiarena.games and it's asking for money, it isn't us. (Some of our own listings link out to third-party sites as paid affiliate placements, and those external sites may ask for money on their own domains — that's a separate matter from whether you're on the genuine hokiarena.games.)

For a broader rundown of red flags beyond these domain checks, see How to Stay Safe Around "HokiArena" Lookalikes.

If you were sent a link

If someone shared a link via WhatsApp, Telegram, SMS, or social media claiming to be "Hoki Arena" and it leads to a request for payment, a login page, or an APK download, it did not come from us. We don't distribute links through messaging apps — the only official entry point is browsing directly to hokiarena.games, or navigating from our own pages.

Why we're addressing this directly

Names like "Hoki" get reused across a lot of unrelated products, including real-money gaming apps. Most of these have no relationship to this directory at all; a small number of our own listings do link out to real-money-adjacent sites as disclosed paid affiliates (see Legalities), which is a different thing from an unrelated app impersonating the Hoki Arena name. If something claiming to be "Hoki Arena" itself doesn't match what's described on this page, it's not us — and we'd genuinely appreciate a heads-up through our contact form if you spot something being passed off as Hoki Arena.
