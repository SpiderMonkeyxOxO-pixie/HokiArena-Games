About Us

A directory built by people who just like games

Hoki Arena started as a simple idea: make it faster to find a decent game to play, without wading through clutter to get there.

What we do

Hoki Arena is an independent directory of casual games. We write short, honest descriptions for every listing so you can browse with a purpose instead of scrolling blindly.

We're not a game studio, a publisher, or a store — we're a directory. Think of us as a well-organized shelf, not the shop itself.

What we don't do

Hoki Arena itself does not offer real-money gambling, betting, or wagering, and we never process deposits or withdrawals ourselves. Some listings are paid affiliate placements that link to independently operated third-party sites, and those sites may include real-money features. See our Legalities page for the full affiliate disclosure.

Because "Hoki" gets reused by a lot of unrelated products, see App Info for what this site actually is, or Verify Official Site if you want to confirm you're in the right place.
