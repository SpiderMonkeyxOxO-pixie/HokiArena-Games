What Hoki Arena Is, and How It Works

Reference page · Updated for 2026

This page exists to answer the questions people most often have before using Hoki Arena for the first time: what it actually is, whether there's anything to install, and how it relates to any other product using a similar name.

What Hoki Arena is

Hoki Arena is a browser-based game directory. We publish listings and short write-ups covering casual games, plus a blog with guides and roundups. You browse it the same way you'd browse any website.

What Hoki Arena is not

Hoki Arena itself is not a gambling app and has no real-money features of its own — we don't run wagering, cash prizes, deposits, or withdrawals. Some of our listings are paid affiliate links to independently operated third-party sites, and those destination sites may include real-money features; see our Legalities page for the full disclosure. If you've come across an app or site using a similar or identical name that we have not listed, that product is not affiliated with this directory. See our guide to verifying you're on the official site if you want to double-check what you're looking at.

Do you need to install anything?

No. There's no app or APK required to use Hoki Arena — it runs entirely in your mobile or desktop browser at hokiarena.games. If a link or message asks you to download an installer file to "access" Hoki Arena, it isn't coming from us. See HokiArena APK: What You Need to Know for more.

Do you need an account or login?

No. Browsing the directory and reading the blog don't require creating an account or signing in. Our contact form is the only place we ask for any information, and only if you choose to reach out. See HokiArena Login: Why There Isn't One for more.
