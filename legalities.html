Legalities

Last updated: 2026

This page explains the general legal position of Hoki Arena ("we", "us", "the site"). It should be read alongside our Privacy Policy and Terms and Conditions.

Nature of this website

Hoki Arena is an informational directory that lists and describes casual games. We publish editorial write-ups and listings; we do not develop, publish, host, or distribute the games we describe.

What Hoki Arena itself does and doesn't do

Hoki Arena itself does not operate any wagering system, game of chance, or betting product, and we do not process deposits, withdrawals, or any exchange of money ourselves. We don't host, run, or control the games we write about.

Affiliate links and paid listings

Some listings include a "Visit Listing" button that is a paid affiliate link: Hoki Arena may earn a referral fee when you click through and sign up on a partner site. These destination sites are operated independently of Hoki Arena. We have not independently verified the gambling status, licensing, or legality of every destination, and some linked sites may offer real-money features such as deposits, withdrawals, or wagering, depending on the site and your location. Before using any linked site: read that site's own terms, and confirm that real-money gaming is legal where you are — rules vary significantly by country and region, and online gambling is restricted or illegal in some jurisdictions. If you are unsure about the legality or nature of a linked site, do not proceed, and contact us if you believe a listing is miscategorized.

Third-party games and links

Where a listing references a game or site available elsewhere, that product is the property of its respective operator, developer, or publisher. We are not responsible for the content, availability, policies, legality, or conduct of third-party games, apps, or websites, including any sites reached via our affiliate links. Visiting, registering on, or transacting with anything from a third party is done entirely at your own discretion, risk, and legal responsibility, and is subject to that party's own terms.

Intellectual property

The Hoki Arena name, logo, layout, and original written content are the property of this site's operator unless otherwise stated. Game names, artwork, and trademarks referenced in our listings belong to their respective owners and are used descriptively, not to imply endorsement or affiliation.

Accuracy of information

We aim to keep listings and articles accurate and up to date, but game details (availability, platforms, features) can change without notice. If you spot outdated or incorrect information, please let us know so we can review it.

Age of use

Hoki Arena is intended for a general audience browsing game information. Some games referenced in our directory may carry their own age ratings set by their publishers — always check a game's own rating and store listing before downloading or playing it.

We may update this page as the site evolves. Continued use of Hoki Arena after changes are posted means you accept the current version.
