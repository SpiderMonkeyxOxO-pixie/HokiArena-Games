Troubleshooting & FAQ

Reference page · Updated for 2026

Answers to the questions we hear most often. If yours isn't covered here, reach out directly.

Access & accounts

Do I need to download an app or APK?

No. Hoki Arena runs entirely in your browser at hokiarena.games — mobile or desktop. There's no installer to download. See What Is HokiArena? or HokiArena APK: What You Need to Know for more detail.

Do I need to create an account or log in?

No. Everything on Hoki Arena — the directory and blog — is open to browse without signing in. We only ever collect information if you choose to use the contact form. See HokiArena Login: Why There Isn't One for more.

The site looks broken or won't load properly on my phone

Try refreshing the page or clearing your browser cache. Hoki Arena is built mobile-first and tested on standard mobile browsers; if something still looks wrong, tell us what device and browser you're using and we'll look into it.

Using the directory

How do I search for a specific game?

On the Games page, use the search box at the top of the listings to filter by name as you type.

A listing looks outdated or incorrect

Listings are reviewed periodically, but details can change on the game's own side. Let us know which listing and what's wrong, and we'll review it.

Can I suggest a game to be added?

Yes — send the game name through our contact form. We review every suggestion before adding it.

About the site itself

Questions about the HokiArena name, whether it's gambling, account/login, or spotting lookalikes are answered in one place in the HokiArena FAQ, with the full overview at What Is HokiArena? and trust/safety detail in Verify Official Site. This page sticks to actual usage problems above.
