Hockey Arena vs. HokiArena: What's the Difference?

Guide · Updated for 2026

If you searched "hockey arena game" or "hockey arena," you likely mean HokiArena — a casual mobile game directory at hokiarena.games. It isn't a hockey game or sports title of any kind; the names just sound alike.

Why the names sound so similar

"Hoki" is Indonesian slang for luck or fortune, and it's pronounced close enough to the English word "hockey" that autocorrect, voice search, and quick typing regularly bridge the two. It's a coincidence of sound, not a connection between the two — HokiArena has no relationship to hockey, ice sports, or sports gaming of any kind.

What HokiArena actually is

HokiArena is a browser-based directory of casual mobile games — short, honest write-ups of each listing, searchable by name, with no account or download required. See What Is HokiArena? for the full explanation, or jump straight to the games directory.

Looking for a "hockey arena app"?

Same answer applies: there's no separate HokiArena app, hockey-themed or otherwise. The directory runs entirely in your browser at hokiarena.games — see HokiArena Mobile App Guide for how it works on a phone.

If you're actually looking for a hockey game

HokiArena's directory is casual games generally, and it doesn't include hockey or other sports titles — so if that's specifically what brought you here, this directory won't have it. No hard feelings; at least now you know the name overlap is just a coincidence, not a dead end on our part.
